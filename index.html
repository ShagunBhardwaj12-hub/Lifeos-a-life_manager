Questions answered

Quick answers about how LifeOS works.

Is the login secure?

It is a demo login. Accounts and hashed passwords are stored only in this browser, so it does not replace a real server, but nobody else can see your data.

Is LifeOS free to try?

Yes. The dashboard above runs entirely in your browser and needs no account.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your browser’s local storage on this device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Is the assistant a real AI?

Yes, on the published page it is powered by Claude once you give permission, and every reply is labelled. If AI is unavailable, for example in a downloaded copy of the file, it uses a clearly labelled demo mode unless you add your own API key in Settings.

How is task priority decided?

With a visible formula: urgency 45%, importance 40% and effort 15%. Every task shows its calculation.

How is the Life Score calculated?

From tasks completed (30%), goal progress (25%), habit consistency (25%) and weekly focus time (20%).
